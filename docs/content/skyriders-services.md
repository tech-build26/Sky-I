## Inspection & Structural Work `/services/inspection-structural/`

### Public copy

**H1:** Inspect closely. Act on what the structure needs.

**Lead:** A remote view can point to a concern; close access helps establish its condition. Skyriders brings inspection and structural work to steel and concrete in places where reaching the work face is part of the challenge.

**Inspection at the work face.** Visual records, photography and video can document the condition of a component. Where the job calls for it, the inspection scope may include ultrasonic wall-thickness or flaw testing, dye penetrant or magnetic particle testing, borescope inspection, and steel or concrete condition surveys. The method should match the material, the suspected defect and the client's inspection brief.

**Concrete assessment and intervention.** Concrete work can begin with visual inspection, cover-meter surveys, carbonation testing or core sampling. The findings may lead to a defined repair, strengthening work, corrosion protection or expansion-joint sealing. Inspection and repair are separate decisions; the scope of intervention follows the condition found and the project specification.

**Where this work fits:** Cooling towers, silos, stacks, steelwork and other industrial structures that need a close view or hands-on intervention.

**Related path:** For an initial visual survey in an enclosed or difficult space, [Sky I](https://skyi.co.za/home/) can help establish where closer investigation may be needed. Drone images do not replace material testing or physical repair.

**Enquiry prompt:** Tell us the structure, the area of concern and any inspection specification already in place. [Discuss an inspection or structural scope](/contact/).

### Editorial depth, not default page copy

Supported methods include visual, photographic, video, borescope/endoscope, ultrasonic wall thickness and flaw testing, dye penetrant, magnetic particle, steel condition, pipe/hanger, structural integrity and coating assessment surveys. Concrete work includes cover-meter surveys, carbonation testing, core drilling, protective coating surveys, corrosion inhibitors, barrier coatings, expansion-joint sealing, concrete repairs and structural strengthening. Use only methods relevant to a specific page module or enquiry; do not display the entire inventory as a dense wall of terms.

## Maintenance & Coatings `/services/maintenance-coatings/`

### Public copy

**H1:** Keep exposed structures serviceable.

**Lead:** Maintenance at height often combines access, preparation and a precise task on the structure. Skyriders works on industrial plant and building fabric where conventional access can be difficult to arrange.

**Maintenance where the work is.** The published service scope includes high-pressure washing, facade maintenance, waterproofing, welding and repairs, duct-component replacement, and silo cleaning and maintenance. Each project needs its own access plan and defined work specification.

**Protective coatings.** Coating work begins with understanding the substrate and its condition. Skyriders lists grit blasting and ultra-high-pressure preparation alongside multi-coat application on steel and concrete structures. The preparation method and coating system should follow the project specification and the service environment; one system is not suitable for every surface.

**Where this work fits:** Tanks, stacks, structural steel, facades, silos and industrial plant components.

**Related path:** When the condition of a surface or component is not yet clear, start with [Inspection & Structural Work](/services/inspection-structural/) to define what needs attention.

**Enquiry prompt:** Share the structure, material, access constraints and the maintenance or coating specification if one exists. [Discuss a maintenance scope](/contact/).

### Editorial depth, not default page copy

The current site also lists structural strengthening, lightning protection system work, snag listing, rock barring and slope stabilisation under maintenance. The brochure adds anchors, deluge-system work, grit blasting and ultra-high-pressure cleaning. These are credible source entries but should be surfaced only when a relevant project, current capability and image support them. Product brand names on the old coatings page should not be reproduced as endorsements or current supply relationships without confirmation.

## Specialist Access & Installations `/services/specialist-access-installations/`

### Public copy

**H1:** Put the right access around the task.

**Lead:** Some work requires more than reaching a height. It may involve positioning components, creating a work platform or planning entry into a restricted space. Skyriders scopes the access method around the structure and the installation or inspection to be completed.

**Installations.** The published service list covers steel erection, rigging, bolting and torque verification, temporary and inspection platforms, nets, and height-safety systems. The exact arrangement depends on the structure, engineering requirements and site constraints.

**Confined spaces.** Skyriders also lists confined-space services for industrial and infrastructure settings. An enquiry should identify the space, entry restrictions, intended task and site requirements so the appropriate access and support scope can be confirmed before work is planned.

**Where this work fits:** Stacks, silos, towers, industrial plant and municipal or construction infrastructure.

**Related path:** If a space needs a visual first look before physical access is defined, [Sky I](https://skyi.co.za/home/) provides a separate drone inspection route. Any later entry or installation remains its own planned scope.

**Enquiry prompt:** Describe the work position, structure and intended task, and note whether entry into a confined space is involved. [Discuss a specialist access scope](/contact/).

### Editorial depth, not default page copy

The current site lists rescue, standby, training and consulting under confined spaces; the brochure mentions consultation and rescue standby. Do not surface these as currently available services until Ishe confirms staffing, scope and any required permissions. The site's training page describes some offerings as future plans, so training must not be presented as an established confined-space service from these sources alone.

## Editorial and publication rules

1. Publish a short lead, two task-focused sections, a few structure examples, one related path and one enquiry action per page. Keep the longer method lists as an editorial reference for later modules, not as default page furniture.
2. Cite Skyriders' own pages and the supplied brochure internally for offered services. External technical sources explain terms and planning considerations; they never prove that Skyriders owns equipment, employs certified personnel or offers a particular service today.
3. Do not publish named clients, site names, identifiable project images, testimonials or project outcomes without explicit clearance. Sector and structure terms can stand on their own.
4. Do not use “certified rope access execution,” “zero scaffolding,” “zero blind spots,” “minimal operational downtime,” current accreditation, years in business or accident record in this copy. Those claims remain unresolved in `docs/research/findings.md`.
5. Before R05 publication, obtain Ishe's confirmation that the named methods and service types remain offered, especially NDT personnel/method scope, structural repairs, coating preparation, and confined-space support. If a capability is no longer current, remove that item without changing the three-page structure.
