# R01 — Skyriders sitemap and content outline

Status: approved structure, revised to three service pages at Ishe's request on 28 September 2026. This defines the full Skyriders site at `ropeaccess.co.za`; the existing `/` pre-landing remains the shared brand choice and `/home/` remains the Skyriders entry. No new page in this outline is yet implemented.

## Navigation and routes

| Navigation label | Route | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `/home/` | Introduce Skyriders' access, inspection and physical intervention work; guide visitors to a relevant service or enquiry. |
| About | `/about/` | Explain the company, the work it undertakes and how it approaches difficult access. |
| Services | `/services/` | Give operations and procurement teams a clear route into three focused service pages. |
| Industries | `/industries/` | Show where those services apply, using sectors rather than client identities. |
| Projects | `/projects/` | Publish only individually approved, anonymised project accounts with usable evidence and imagery. |
| Safety & Quality | `/safety-quality/` | Explain the working approach and publish only current, verified credentials. |
| Contact | `/contact/` | Provide direct contact details and a scoped enquiry route. |

The seven labels are the primary navigation. The Skyriders identity links to `/home/`; the shared pre-landing remains reachable through a clearly labelled brand switch. A persistent link to Sky I's `/home/` should identify it as the drone inspection site. Direct deep links bypass the pre-landing. Do not add empty Training, News, Careers or Downloads top-level pages merely to mirror the old site.

Each service page follows a shared outline: the problem or structure; its two clearly labelled service areas; representative tasks; how inspection leads to access or repair where applicable; relevant sectors; verified image or project link; and a specific enquiry path. The `/services/` index offers short, practical summaries of the three pages. Drone inspection is linked to Sky I rather than presented as an indistinguishable Skyriders service page.

## Page outlines

### Home `/home/`

1. Lead with industrial rope access and the relationship between inspection, access and work on the structure.
2. Show the three service groups through an editorial overview, with direct links to their pages.
3. Explain the work sequence: understand the structure, plan access, inspect or intervene, report what was done. This is an editorial framework, not a promise that every job follows identical steps.
4. Feature two or three sectors with links to Industries.
5. Feature an approved project only when its facts and images are cleared; otherwise use a service-led composition.
6. Link to Sky I when a remote first look is useful, then end with a contact route.

### About `/about/`

1. Identify Skyriders Access Specialists (Pty) Ltd and its rope access focus.
2. Describe the types of work and environments represented by the verified service inventory.
3. Explain the complementary Sky I relationship without implying a shared legal identity until confirmed.
4. Provide a clear path to Safety & Quality and Contact. Founding year, staff counts, experience totals and ownership claims remain absent until verified.

### Industries `/industries/`

1. Explain that service methods adapt to the structure and operating environment.
2. Cover power generation, mining, petroleum and oil and gas, construction, facilities maintenance, telecommunications, municipal infrastructure, and buildings and roofing where supported by specific service examples.
3. Link each sector to relevant services without claiming a named client or a project outcome.
4. Keep “Households” in the existing pre-landing strip as requested, but do not create a household service section until Ishe confirms the offer.

### Projects `/projects/`

1. Introductory note on the type of work represented, followed by approved case studies only.
2. A project detail route `/projects/[slug]/` uses: anonymous sector and structure, the task, the approach, the completed work or observation, approved imagery, and related services.
3. Do not publish client names, site names, locations, logos, or identifying image details without explicit clearance. Do not imply measured savings, safety outcomes or performance improvements without evidence.
4. If no case study is cleared, keep this route unpublished and remove its navigation item until content exists; do not display an empty grid or fabricated example.

### Safety & Quality `/safety-quality/`

1. Explain project planning, access methods, supervision, inspection and reporting in plain terms once Skyriders confirms its current process.
2. Show current certifications, memberships or downloadable certificates only after checking validity, scope and publication permission.
3. Avoid zero-incident, “certified” or statutory-compliance claims without current evidence. Do not reproduce the brochure's historical legal summary as current advice.

### Contact `/contact/`

1. Offer telephone and email directly, with office details once Ishe confirms which addresses are public and current.
2. Enquiry fields should capture name, organisation, reply address, work type, site/region and a short scope. Allow visitors to describe a request without exposing private project details publicly.
3. Explain response handling and privacy in owner-approved wording once the delivery method is known. Do not show a form success state before real delivery is implemented and verified.

## Company profile download

Place a downloadable profile on About and, if useful, on Contact **only after** a public-safe PDF is prepared and approved. The supplied 2026 brochure is a research source and contains client marks, project identifiers and claims that need review; it must not be linked as-is. If no cleared PDF is ready, omit the download link while retaining the rest of the pages.
