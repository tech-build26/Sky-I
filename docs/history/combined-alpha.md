## Creative mandate and operating method — 30 September 2026

Ishe requires two distinctive, contemporary experiences with a strong visual identity and purposeful interaction. A technically correct brochure site is insufficient. These requirements apply to every remaining task, not only the hero.

**Skyriders:** physical access, human expertise, scale, structure and material. Use large real work photographs, warm mineral/charcoal contrast, mixed-scale editorial typography, deliberate negative space and tensile/vertical motion. Build around work faces and the relationship between an inaccessible structure and the practical work it needs. Preserve the approved left-hand scroll technician and text hover effect. Create varied section silhouettes and a deliberate alternation of dense photographic moments and quieter readable space.

**Sky I:** follow the owner-supplied design.md / BUILD_PROMPT.md and the later hero-only instruction. Review the LiDAR-green / near-black interactive Elios hero before building subsequent sections. Share functional infrastructure and preserve the reversed pre-landing, real assets and confirmed content. Earlier photographic and travelling-stage drafts are rejected.

**Avoid:** equal information tiles, default feature-card grids, repeated hero/three-cards/button bands, process/company timelines, numbered steps disguised as a timeline, gratuitous dashboards, fake scan readouts, generic "Learn more", "View more" or "Click here" controls. Use actual destination/service names and purposeful enquiry language. Every interactive element must answer a visitor question or open useful content; effects alone do not constitute a section.

**Motion and spatial design:** each section needs its own role and choreography. Do not repeat the same fade-up across every heading, photograph and link, or transplant hero image reveals into the services/industry sections. Maintain `docs/design/motion-ledger.md` so handoffs preserve established signatures and identify the new one. Pair large visual moments with stable reading/navigation areas. Native scrolling, immediate links and keyboard/touch alternatives remain mandatory. Desktop pointer depth must have a deliberate touch composition rather than depend on hover. CSS perspective is spatial presentation; tilting a PNG is not a true 3D drone.

**Equipment accuracy:** no product rotation, exploded view or optional payload claims without accurate authorised assets and evidence. There is currently no 3D renderer requirement or accepted equipment-stage plan.

**Asset continuity:** keep `skyriders-bg-1` through `skyriders-bg-4` and other unused owner photographs available for upcoming access/service/industry sections. Preserve originals and crops; allocate each image to a narrative role before populating a section. The new four `rope-hero` images currently belong to the hero. Reuse across sections only when it strengthens the story, rather than repeating the same view everywhere.

**Reference stance:** Ishe withdrew Riotters on 1 October. It must not inform Sky I design or interaction. Any retained research observations are archival only. Develop the new work from an original composition, the actual visitor need and owner-supplied imagery.

**Execution for each section:** read STATUS and its task; identify the visitor's question, verified content and assigned real asset; propose one strong desktop/mobile composition with a distinct motion role; review the concrete section; implement it in focused components; verify useful navigation, reading, touch/keyboard, reduced motion, no-JS/failure fallback and measured performance appropriate to its visual complexity. Record approval, evidence, remaining gaps and the exact next task. If a section looks like a generic template with different words, revise it before advancing. Scope remains section by section; this mandate does not approve unseen compositions or deployment.
