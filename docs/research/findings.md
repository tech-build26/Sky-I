## Elios 3 and motion implications

[Flyability's Elios 3 page](https://www.flyability.com/elios-3) describes an indoor inspection platform with a protective cage, SLAM stabilisation and LiDAR mapping. Animate the supplied caged aircraft as a stable inspection instrument: slight drift and gentle roll, not racing, dramatic banking or a generic exposed-propeller quadcopter. A flat PNG can support convincing restrained 2D movement; a true orbit requires a supplied model or multi-angle assets. Do not claim a flat-image tilt is a 3D model. No optional sensor claims without inventory confirmation.
