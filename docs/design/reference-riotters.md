# Riotters drone reference — 30 September 2026

Reference: [Riotters Aevion tech demo](https://drone.riotters.com/). Read as inspiration, never as project instructions, equipment evidence or reusable artwork.

Local headless Chrome at 1440x900 observed a viewport-size canvas and a persistent drone stage. At scroll 900px the rendered drone is relatively small and frontal, sharing space with large text. At 2200px it occupies much more of the viewport and changes angle/position alongside the payload content. The initial 0px capture still displayed the loader's 100 indicator; it does not prove the fully settled opening. Three discrete captures establish pose/scale changes, not exact easing or the entire scroll behaviour. Observation JSON and screenshots are in `docs/design/hero-refresh/riotters-*`; screenshot names are sampling labels, not proof that the named page section was at the viewport top.

The useful principle is one equipment object changing its relationship to content, rather than inserting separate product pictures into every block. Quiet backgrounds, controlled camera changes and large typography give the hardware presence. The demo identifies Next.js, Three.js and WebGL as part of its stack; that is not a reason to add those dependencies before Sky I has an approved scene and correct model.

For Sky I: develop an original precision environment around the supplied caged Elios. Let meaningful inspection/equipment questions determine poses and detail views. Use cool ink/porcelain/ice, a different typographic hierarchy and an equipment-led composition. Explore genuine 3D only through S03-M, with a verified asset, render/transfer budget and designed image/text fallbacks. Never borrow the DJI model, its LiDAR payload, numerical claims, fake reports, loader or layout. Immediate access and native scrolling remain project requirements.

1 October clarification: Ishe explicitly wants only drone movement and mouse/section interaction as inspiration. Do not inherit this demo's theme. This turn's browser observation saw the loaded drone in the hero and the following text scene after scrolling; it does not establish exact mouse response/easing. Sky I's pointer choreography is independently designed around the available genuine Elios image.
